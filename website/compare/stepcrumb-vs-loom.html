Questions

Is StepCrumb a Loom alternative?

For how-to content, yes. Loom records a video of your screen; StepCrumb records your clicks and turns them into a written guide, an SOP, a training page and a narrated video with auto-zoom. For video messages with your face and comments, Loom is the better fit.

Does StepCrumb upload my recordings?

No. StepCrumb saves screenshots and videos in your browser on your computer. It goes online only to check a Pro license key and, unless you switch it off, to send anonymous counts such as "1 guide made", never what you capture.

Can I make a video with StepCrumb?

Yes. Every capture can become a narrated video that zooms where you clicked, with captions and a voice made on your computer. Videos up to 5 minutes are free; Pro removes the limit and adds 1080p.

How much does StepCrumb cost compared with Loom?

StepCrumb is free, with Pro at $8 a month, $69 a year or $49 once for lifetime access. Loom's pricing page lists Business at $18 and Business + AI at $24 per user per month.

Why a written guide instead of a video?

People can skim steps, search them, copy them into a wiki and follow them at their own pace. With StepCrumb you don't have to choose: the same capture gives you both.
