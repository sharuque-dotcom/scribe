Questions

Is StepCrumb a good Tango alternative?

Yes, if you make step-by-step guides on your own or in a small team and care about privacy. StepCrumb gives you unlimited guides, PDF export, automatic blurring, narrated video and walkthroughs on the live website on its free plan, needs no account and keeps everything on your computer. If you need a shared team workspace or SSO, Tango fits better.

Does StepCrumb upload my screenshots?

No. StepCrumb saves screenshots and videos in your browser on your computer. It goes online only to check a Pro license key and, unless you switch it off, to send anonymous counts such as "1 guide made", never what you capture.

Does Tango blur personal data automatically?

According to Tango's pricing page, automatic blurring of personal data is part of its Enterprise plan, and manual blurring is on Pro. StepCrumb blurs emails, phone numbers, card numbers, Emirates ID numbers and IBANs automatically on every plan.

How much does StepCrumb cost compared with Tango?

StepCrumb is free, with Pro at $8 a month, $69 a year or $49 once for lifetime access. Tango Pro is $22 per user per month billed yearly for one or two users, or $15 per user per month billed yearly for three or more.

How many guides can I make for free?

As many as you like with StepCrumb. Tango's free plan holds 5 workflows in the team library.
