Questions

Is StepCrumb a good Guidde alternative?

Yes, if you want how-to videos and written guides without uploading anything. StepCrumb makes a narrated video with auto-zoom plus a guide, an SOP and a training page from one click-through, with automatic blurring and PDF export on the free plan. If you need a shared video library with sharing links, Guidde fits better.

Does StepCrumb upload my recordings?

No. StepCrumb saves screenshots and videos in your browser on your computer. It goes online only to check a Pro license key and, unless you switch it off, to send anonymous counts such as "1 guide made", never what you capture.

Does StepCrumb have AI voiceover?

Yes. StepCrumb reads your steps out loud with a natural voice that runs on your computer, on every plan. Nothing is sent to a voice service.

How much does StepCrumb cost compared with Guidde?

StepCrumb is free, with Pro at $8 a month, $69 a year or $49 once for lifetime access. Guidde's pricing page lists Pro at $19 and Business at $39 per creator per month, billed yearly.

Can StepCrumb export a PDF for free?

Yes. Every guide, SOP and training page exports to PDF on the free plan, with a small StepCrumb footer. Pro removes the footer and adds Word, HTML and Markdown.
